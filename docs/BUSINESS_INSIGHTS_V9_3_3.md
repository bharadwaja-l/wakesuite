# Business Insights — V9.3.3

Business Insights mirrors the Marketplace Insights visual framework while remaining focused on decision support.

View switch:
- Insights
- Detailed Data
- History

Pricing Insights uses price movement, observed revenue response, inventory interference, suppression/Buy Box interference, fulfilment and decision signals.

Inventory Insights uses OOS, DRR, Days of Cover, inventory risk, potential OOS loss, restock recovery, possible overstock and marketplace imbalance.

Revenue response is observed following a price change; WakeSuite does not claim causality.
